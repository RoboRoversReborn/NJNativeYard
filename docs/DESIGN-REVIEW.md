# Two bounded design and educational reviews — 4 November 2026

These reviews inspected source, component behavior and CSS. They do not constitute rendered visual approval.

| Homeowner criterion | Result after revisions |
| --- | --- |
| Understand the benefit | Homepage leads with understanding and improving the yard ecosystem. |
| Know where to begin | Primary scan action and working three-yard preview; full gallery follows explanatory context. |
| Explore the photograph | Compact score precedes the photo; category filters and map/list selection remain coordinated. |
| Understand the estimate | Plant-region percentages are separate from signed score points, deductions and shared bonuses. |
| Learn something useful | Recognition, sourced seasonal interest and available next steps have progressive disclosure. |

Cycle one corrected promises of instant analysis and universal seasonal care, replaced an obsolete source note, and connected curated observation prompts to the existing close-up tool. Four source-guarded stories lead with memorable plant details. The first curated seasonal-action collection covers 19 sample profiles, not every catalog entry.

Cycle two corrected the directions to the Care & seasons tab and moved keyboard focus into the comparison/close-up workspace, restoring focus to plant details on return. These actions do not automatically confirm a plant identity.

All five category cards wrap without a hidden horizontal rail. Saving calls to action reflect account availability; guests can scan, compare and print. Individual care/control sources retain their scope, and control instructions require identity review.

Remaining gate: rendered desktop/mobile checks and independent functional retesting of the final stable build. Source review, DOM tests and successful builds alone do not establish those results.
